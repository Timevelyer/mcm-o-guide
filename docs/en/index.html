    Why these seven thresholds

    Each one is taken from the distribution in the corpus, not from a list of "best practices".

    01 / NUMBERS

    Number density

    Outstanding summaries carry 20–35 concrete numbers; Meritorious ones 10–15. This is the most stable gap between the two tiers.

    02 / TASKS

    Task coverage

    Every sub-problem gets at least one sentence of method plus a quantitative result. A missing task is a direct elimination in the second round; roughly 25% of teams do it.

    03 / LENGTH

    Length

    Median 437 words, interquartile range 350–520. Anything past 600 usually means too much background.

    04 / KEYWORDS

    Keywords

    Four or five, and one of them must be your model name — a memorable label such as GIID or BDS.

    05 / SENSITIVITY

    Sensitivity

    The line that recurs in judges' commentaries: teams that do sensitivity properly almost always stand out.

    06 / LIMITS

    Limitations

    End on solid ground: strengths, limitations, conclusion. Two of the three beats a conclusion alone.

    Install it into your agent

    The guide ships as a skill pack. Once installed it is picked up when you draft, revise or audit a paper. You can also just read it as a handbook.

    git clone https://github.com/Timevelyer/mcm-o-guide.git
mv mcm-o-guide ~/.config/opencode/skills/mcm-o-guide   # OpenCode
mv mcm-o-guide ~/.claude/skills/mcm-o-guide            # or Claude Code
    On Windows use Move-Item. Entry point is SKILL.md; the English edition is SKILL.en.md.

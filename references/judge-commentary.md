## 1. 2009 Problem A — Judges' Commentary (Kelly Black, Clarkson University)

### 1.1 评审流程

| 轮次 | 评委组 | 关注点 |
|---|---|---|
| **Initial Grading** | 独立初审组 | 每篇至少 2 人读；一致低分直接淘汰；不确定则放行 |
| **Screening Round** | 第二组评委 | 是否满足高分最低要求；标准越来越严 |
| **Final Rounds** | 全体评委 | 每篇多轮阅读；关注每一步的一致性；保持高分才能前进 |

> 当年 A 题共 4 篇 Outstanding。

### 1.2 核心评判标准（逐条提取）

#### 摘要（Summary）
- **原文：** *"The summary is the first thing that a judge will read. It is the first impression, and it imparts the expectations for the whole paper."*
- **原文：** *"It is absolutely vital that a paper have a complete and well written summary to make it past the initial rounds."*
- **原文：** *"It is also vital that the details in the summary be consistent with the rest of the paper especially in the later rounds."*
- **原文：** *"Many teams find it tempting to include a large amount of background information or provide clever narratives motivating the problem. Unfortunately, such material in the summary can drastically reduce the amount of space available to discuss the team's results and discussion of the approach."*

**提炼：摘要 = 问题概述 + 具体结论 + 方法概览。不要背景铺垫，不要叙事，不要占空间。**

#### 目标定义
- **原文：** *"The goal for this problem is to find a way to move vehicles through a traffic circle in an optimal way. [...] What is not clear is what 'best' might mean. This key aspect was left open for the students to decide what it means. [...] the students were required to make it clear in their report how they themselves interpreted this part of the problem."*
- **原文：** *"Teams that managed to maintain some level of consistency stood out from the entries and tended to elicit a more positive response from the judges."*

**提炼：开放式目标必须在论文中明确定义，且后续评估必须与定义一致。**

#### 灵敏度分析
- **原文：** *"Every year the judges explicitly discuss the issue of sensitivity and stability. The few teams that make a concerted effort to explore this aspect of their model will almost always stand out, and this part of their efforts will be seen in a positive light."*
- **原文：** *"Exploration of the sensitivity of a model can be as simple as exploring what happens for a different ranges in a parameter to the use of more sophisticated methodologies such as an exploration of a sensitivity matrix."*

**提炼：灵敏度是评委每年必讨论的话题，做了就一定加分。简单扫参数范围就够了。**

#### 图表
- **原文：** *"It is not uncommon to see entries in which figures and tables are included but have no detailed discussion associated with them. The teams need to make the effort to integrate the figures and tables into their discussion."*
- **原文：** *"The figures and tables are part of the story the students need to tell and are not something that stands apart from their discussion."*

**提炼：图表不是装饰，是叙事的一部分。必须配解读段落。**

#### 一致性
- **原文：** *"A team that produces an entry that has an approach that is consistent with its summary and the assumptions are consistent throughout the paper greatly improves the chances that the judges will have a positive reaction when we read it."*

**提炼：摘要-假设-模型-结果-结论必须贯穿一致。**

#### 仿真与理论验证
- **原文：** *"The few teams that did provide a confirmation of the numerical model made an immediate positive impression."*
- **原文：** *"Teams that were able to tie together the theoretical analysis of their model along with their numerical trials received immediate positive attention."*

**提炼：数值仿真 + 理论分析对比 = 立即可见的正面印象。**

## 2. 2009 Problem B — Judges' Commentary (Marie Vanisko, Carroll College)

### 2.1 核心评判标准

#### 摘要
- **原文：** *"The Executive Summary is often still below par; the Summary should motivate the reader to read the paper. It must not merely restate the problem, but indicate how it was modeled and what conclusions were reached, without being unduly technical."*

**提炼：摘要 ≠ 复述题目。摘要 = 方法 + 结论。不要过于技术化。**

#### 假设
- **原文：** *"Assumptions must be clearly stated and justified where appropriate. Some teams overlook important factors and/or make unrealistic assumptions with no rationale. It should be made clear in the model precisely where those assumptions are used."*

**提炼：假设必须有理由，且必须在模型中明确指出在哪里使用了该假设。**

#### 引用与数据来源
- **原文：** *"The best papers not only list trustworthy resources but also document their use throughout the paper."*

**提炼：不仅要有参考文献列表，还要在正文中标注每条文献被用在何处。**

#### O 奖门槛
- **原文：** *"To receive an Outstanding or Meritorious designation, teams had to address issues raised in each of these Requirements."*
- **原文：** *"Additionally, outstanding papers considered both wireless and wired landlines and the infrastructure to support cell phones and landlines."*

**提炼：O 奖 = 全覆盖所有 Requirements + 额外深度。**

## 3. 2021 MCM-ICM: Procedures and Tips (COMAP 官方)

### 3.1 优秀论文特征

- **原文：** *"The best contest papers present complete and logical analysis in an organized and clear presentation above and beyond simply addressing the requirements."*
- **原文：** *"These papers are easy to read, easy to follow, logical, and comprehensive."*
- **原文：** *"These papers will usually include sections that address assumptions with justifications, modeling process(es), results of modeling/analysis, strengths and weaknesses, sensitivity, and conclusions."*
- **原文：** *"These papers have in line documentation, footnotes, or endnotes with associated references."*

**提炼：优秀论文 = 完整 + 逻辑清晰 + 易读 + 覆盖 Requirements + 假设带理由 + 灵敏度 + 优劣势 + 引用。**

### 3.2 题目类型区分

| 题目 | 类型 |
|---|---|
| MCM A | Continuous |
| MCM B | Discrete |
| MCM C | Data Insights |
| ICM D | Operations Research / Network Science |
| ICM E | Environmental Science |
| ICM F | Policy |

### 3.3 提交合规

- 最大 25 页（含摘要/目录/附录/参考文献）
- 12 号字体，英文
- PDF < 17MB
- 页眉：`Team # [队号] Page [页码] of [总页数]`
- 无个人信息
- 文件名 = 队号.pdf
- 邮件主题 = 队号
- 收件人：solutions@comap.com

## 4. 评委点评交叉分析

### 4.1 评委年年必提的主题

| 主题 | 提及次数 | 评委原话摘要 |
|---|---|---|
| **摘要质量** | 3/3 | 第一印象，决定能否过初筛 |
| **全文一致性** | 3/3 | 摘要-假设-模型-结果必须一致 |
| **灵敏度分析** | 3/3 | 做了就加分，简单扫参数也行 |
| **图表解读** | 2/3 | 图表必须融入叙事，不能孤立 |
| **假设合理性** | 2/3 | 必须有理由，必须在模型中体现 |
| **引用规范** | 2/3 | 好的引用 = 列表 + 正文标注 |
| **覆盖所有 Requirements** | 2/3 | 遗漏 = 直接降档 |

### 4.2 评委最看重的 3 件事

1. **摘要**（决定是否愿意继续读）
2. **一致性**（决定是否信任你的分析）
3. **灵敏度**（决定是否认为你的模型可靠）

### 4.3 O 奖 vs 其他奖的关键差距

评委暗示的 O 奖特征：
- 不止于回答 Requirements，还有额外洞察
- 理论分析 + 数值仿真双重验证
- 灵敏度分析深入（不仅是扫参数，还有解读）
- 图表与正文深度融合（不是孤立的"见图 X"）
- 引用贯穿全文（不只是最后的列表）
- 假设使用在正文中被显式引用
